/03 How it works
how-it-works.html writing

Three steps. Simple.

  1. You tell me about your business.

    A 15-minute call. I ask what you sell, who buys it, and what you want visitors to do. No prep on your side. Just show up and answer.

  2. We make the page.

    Over the next seven days, we design and write your page. Halfway through, you see a preview link. You send us everything you want changed in one batch.

  3. You go live.

    After your changes, you approve. The page goes up at the address you want. You pay the second half, and I send you the source files plus a one-page guide for editing things later.

Frequently asked

Plain answers.

Real questions, written out the way we'd explain them on a call.

  • Q. How much does this cost, total?

    Three flat-fee plans: Launch Page ($397), Growth Site ($597), Market Dominator ($1,097), plus maintenance credit packs starting at $49 per small update. That's the total. No monthly platform fees, no card on file. You pay half to start, half when the page is approved.

  • Q. How long does it actually take?

    Seven days from the moment your deposit clears to the page being live on the address you want. That includes time for you to review a draft and ask for changes. Need it faster? Rush delivery (72h) is +$180.

  • Q. Will my page work on phones?

    Yes. Designed for phones first, then adapted to bigger screens. Most of your visitors will see it on a phone, and the design is built around that.

  • Q. I don't have a website yet. Do I need to buy anything?

    You'll want a domain name, which is your web address, like yourbusiness.com. Domains cost about $15 a year, bought from a registrar like Namecheap or GoDaddy.

    If you don't have one yet, you can skip it for now and use a free address like yourbusiness.netlify.app. Hosting (the place the page actually lives on the internet) is free with the services we use. You don't pay anything ongoing.

  • Q. I already have a website. Will I lose my old one?

    Not unless you want to. Two options. Option one, we replace your old site by pointing your existing address at the new page. The swap takes a few minutes and I walk you through it. Option two, we put the new page at a separate address and you decide later. Your old site stays up either way until you say otherwise.

  • Q. What if I want changes after I see it?

    One round of changes is included in Launch Page and Growth Site. Two rounds in Market Dominator. You see a preview, send us everything you want adjusted in one batch, and we make the changes. No nickel-and-diming for tiny edits. Typos, color tweaks, headline rewrites all count as part of the round.

  • Q. What if I just don't like it?

    If you've sent feedback and the revised version still doesn't work for you, you get the second half of your money back. You keep what you've already paid for. Files, design, copy. No fight.

    This rarely happens. If there's a mismatch, it usually shows up at the discovery call before money changes hands.

  • Q. Can I edit the page myself later?

    Yes. You get all the source files and a one-page guide. Small changes (text, photos, prices) you can do yourself by editing the file in any text editor.

    Bigger changes, like new sections or totally different layouts, can be done by any web developer. The files are standard HTML and CSS, not locked into a platform like Squarespace or Wix.

  • Q. Do you have a referral program?

    Yes. Every past client gets a unique referral code in their goodbye email. If they pass it to someone who hires me, the new client gets $50 off their project and the past client banks $50 toward any future PageForge work (maintenance, a new project, a refresh, anything). No expiry. Stack as many as you want. New clients drop the code in the contact form below.

  • Q. Can you guarantee my page will get me customers?

    No, and anyone who promises that is selling you something. What I can promise is a fast, well-designed page focused on the one thing you want visitors to do. The rest depends on traffic, your offer, your audience, and timing.

    That said, a clear page focused on a single action almost always outperforms a busy page trying to do five things. If your page is currently doing five things, the math is on your side.

  • Q. Why is this cheaper than an agency?

    Because we run lean. No project managers, no junior designers, no account team you've never met. Less overhead means a lower price for you. The output isn't lower quality. It's a leaner setup.

    The trade-off is that we take a small number of clients at a time. If we're at capacity, we hold a slot for you on a future date. No payment up front for that, just a date. Most of the time it's a few days.
